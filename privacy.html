Privacy policy

Ozzzz Publisher. Last updated 18 November 2026.

What this app is

Ozzzz Publisher is a private publishing tool operated by prod. Ozzzz. It posts videos, captions and thumbnails that prod. Ozzzz created to the Instagram account @prod.ozzzz and the YouTube channel @prodozzzz. It has no other users.

Data it accesses

  • Instagram (Meta): basic profile information, the ability to publish content, and post insights for @prod.ozzzz only.
  • YouTube (Google): the ability to upload and edit videos and read channel information for @prodozzzz only.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens are stored only in the operator's local device keychain. The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer personal data of any other person, and it does not use data for advertising. Insights are read only to measure the performance of prod. Ozzzz's own posts.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time: in Instagram under Settings, Apps and websites, and in your Google Account under Security, Third-party access. See data deletion for how stored tokens are deleted.
